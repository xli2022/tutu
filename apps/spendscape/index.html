Where do I find a statement file?

Sign in to your bank's website and open an account's activity or statements page. Look for Download or Export, pick a date range, and choose CSV (sometimes called "Excel" or "spreadsheet"), QFX/OFX ("Quicken" or "Money") or QIF.

PDF statements can't be read here: they are laid out for printing, not for computers. Most banks offer one of the formats above right next to the PDF.

Any spreadsheet with a date, a description and an amount column works too, with the columns in any order. Debit and credit in separate columns is fine, and so are decimal commas.
